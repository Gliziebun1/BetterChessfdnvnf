      Free online chess with Elo matchmaking

      BetterChess is a free chess game that runs in your browser. Choose Play online, pick a starting rating and press Find a match: you are paired with another player at a similar Elo rating. The search range starts tight and widens the longer you wait, up to the limit you choose. Your rating updates after every online game.

      Pick a time control before you queue, from 1-minute bullet and 3-minute blitz to 10-minute rapid and 30-minute classical (or play with no clock), and you are only paired with players searching for the same time.

      Prefer to practise? Play the computer at five levels from 400 to 2000 Elo, solve mate-in-one and mate-in-two puzzles, or load a game into the analysis board and step through it with an engine evaluation. No account, no download.

      Is BetterChess free?

      Yes. There is nothing to sign up for or pay for.

      How does matchmaking work?

      Players in the queue are matched with the closest rating inside a range that grows over time.

      Which time controls are available?

      Bullet (1 min, 2|1), blitz (3 min, 3|2, 5 min), rapid (10 min, 15|10), classical (30 min) and unlimited. The clock starts once both players have made their first move.

      Does it follow the real rules?

      Yes: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and stalemate are all supported.
