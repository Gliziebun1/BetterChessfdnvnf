      Free online chess with Elo matchmaking

      BetterChess is a free chess game that runs in your browser. Click Play online, pick a starting rating and press Find a match: you are paired with another player searching at a similar Elo rating. The search range starts tight and widens the longer you wait, up to the limit you choose. Your rating updates after every online game.

      Prefer to practise? Play the computer at five levels from 400 to 2000 Elo, undo moves, flip the board, and customise board and piece styles. There is no account, no download and no clock.

      Is BetterChess really free?

      Yes. There is nothing to sign up for or pay for.

      How does the matchmaking work?

      Players in the queue are matched with the closest rating inside a range that grows over time, so you get a fair game quickly.

      Does it follow the real rules?

      Yes: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and stalemate are all supported.
